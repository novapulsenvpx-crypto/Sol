How Tokens Actually Work

This is NOT a pre-minted token. This is a demand-driven treasury release model — the fairest token distribution ever built.

🏛️ The NovaPulse Treasury

At deployment, all 21,000,000 NVPX are minted once into the smart contract itself — which IS the treasury. The deployer receives zero tokens. No team wallet. No allocation. The contract owns everything. After goLive() is called, even the deployer loses all control. The treasury runs itself.

No Rewards on Sells
